Why this moment matters: it's the known weak spot of pay-at-table.

This is a known risk across the category, not a known qlub bug.

  • The industry names it as where QR payment breaks.Setup guides list uneven splits, disputed items and payments tried twice. One describes a guest paying by QR while a server brings a card machine for another guest, where slow status updates risk double handling. [1] [2]
  • Competitors now sell the fix as a feature.HungryPay says staff see the outstanding balance while guests pay by phone and through a server, and Paybyrd lists partial payments and mixed settlement. [3] [4]
  • For qlub, mixed tables are likely common.A qlub product leader reports SoftPOS reached 30% of payment value in Singapore and 50% in Australia. That mixed tables follow is my inference, not their figure. [5]

How I'd test it: qlub's own data first, then the restaurant floor.

  • Size it: what share of tables are paid with more than one method, and how long they take from first payment to closed check.
  • Watch it: shadow three busy dinner services, in a high-SoftPOS market and a UAE venue, and follow every mixed table.
  • Decide: if mixed tables are rare or already close cleanly, drop it and move to the next problem.

What I'd measure

  • Mixed tables closed without staff stepping in
  • Time from first payment to closed check
  • Double-payment refunds

I haven't used qlub's mixed-table flow, and competitors already advertise mixed settlement, so qlub may handle this well today. The test is how it holds up on a busy Friday night.

My research plan

  • Who: 8 to 10 merchants across high and low SoftPOS markets, 6 to 8 servers, 10 diners.
  • How: shadow services, short interviews, review and ticket tagging, device activity by venue.
  • Output: a readout on what's true, what isn't, and what the revamp should do first.

Questions I'd ask

  • MerchantWalk me through closing tables on your last busy Friday.
  • MerchantWhat would make you stop using SoftPOS?
  • ServerWhen do you reach for your phone instead of pointing to the QR?
  • ServerTell me about the last payment that went wrong.
  • DinerWhat made you hand over your card instead of scanning?

Also worth exploring: three smaller threads.

Charges shown late

qlub's rewards app is rated 3.0, and one August 2026 review says tips and service charges appeared only at the payment step. [10]

Show every charge on the first screen, with the tip clearly optional.

Proof at venue level

With sunday planning a UAE launch, restaurants will compare providers more closely. [7]

A monthly report from each venue's own numbers: tips, time to pay, payment mix.

Staff confidence

Handheld devices staff don't trust tend to go unused, and phones add battery and connection risks. [11] [12]

A practice mode, a "my tips today" view, and a device check before service.
